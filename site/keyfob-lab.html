SASORI / PHONE-ONLY EXPERIMENT

Does the phone hear your key fob?

Measure actual button clicks, compare them with unrelated noises, and export the evidence. No extra hardware.

Important: This listens to the phone microphone, not the 433 MHz radio signal. A detected click does not mean the phone decoded, copied, or controlled a car remote. Test only your own equipment; don't test near an active vehicle.

STEP 1

Set up the microphone

Open this page directly in Safari over HTTPS. Tap Start, then click the fob once. The live sound meter should react instantly — no calibration or trial is required.

Move LEFT to hear quieter clicks (but more false alarms). Move RIGHT to reject background sounds. The working ClapSplash site uses the same frequency-energy approach, with a default threshold of 100.

STEP 2

Run controlled trials

First confirm Live clicks detected increases when you click the fob. Then choose a condition. Tap Run 1 trial, wait for the 3-second countdown, then do it once during the 2-second GO window. Keep the phone still.
