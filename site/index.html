Your first iPhone inspection

            
    
              
  1. 
                Open this HTTPS page in Bluefy. Enable Bluetooth, power on your own Elmo, and
                keep it nearby. Close other apps connected to it.
              
    2. 
              
  2. 
                Load the published Elmo UUIDs. Confirm the source notice and authorization
                checkbox. Keep simulation off.
              
    3. 
              
  3. 
                Tap Connect & inspect. Select Elmo L2L. Keep the page visible; the app stops
                after 15 seconds from GATT connection.
              
    4. 
              
  4. 
                Review and save the result. Look for observed characteristics, notification
                counts, and the disconnect reason. No messages is an inconclusive result.
              
    5. 
            

            

              Stop and switch off physical power if there is unexpected motion, heat, binding,
              smoke, or exposed wiring. A browser disconnect cannot guarantee physical motion stops.
