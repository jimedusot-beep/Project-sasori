Restore failed, data missing, or another tab changed a save?

Keep the original file and browser data. Check the file type, format/version, storage limit and destination workspace. A validation failure must not partially replace saved work. Read that workspace’s recovery message and use a fresh copy of the original backup.

If another tab changed a save, export your work if possible, reload and retry from the latest version. Do not clear browser storage to resolve a conflict. Pages, the native app, Bluefy, Safari and a local companion may have separate stores.

Invalid records stay in their original store until you explicitly choose a recovery action. This overview never deletes or repairs them automatically.

Automatic private cross-device sync is future work. It needs authentication, per-owner authorization, encryption, conflict handling, deletion and recovery. These manual exports do not implement sync.
