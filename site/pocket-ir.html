OPEN-HARDWARE EXPERIMENT 002

Your phone. Your remote.
No computer companion.

This opt-in pilot talks directly to a specifically flashed, owner-controlled ESP32-S3 infrared bridge. It cannot send IR from an ordinary phone and is not verified against physical hardware in Sasori.

Build details and limits ↗ · Upstream firmware reference ↗

01 / Pair your bridge

Use Bluefy on iPhone or a browser supporting Web Bluetooth. The bridge must advertise the documented service UUID. No network, cloud account or companion process is used.

Disconnected. No Bluetooth chooser has opened.

Closing this page, hiding it, or disconnecting stops new requests. IR already emitted cannot be recalled.

02 / Configure one named slot

Use a code from your own remote capture or a checked, model-specific source. No universal scan, guessing, or arbitrary packet console.

The firmware stores slots in flash. Selecting Configure changes that slot, not Elmo or other Bluetooth devices. Wait for a Stored notification; no automatic sending occurs.

03 / Test deliberately

Configure and receive a saved acknowledgment for a slot during this connection before sending.

The bridge may report “Transmit attempted”; that does not prove the target responded. Watch the real device, and never use this experiment with access controls, vehicle systems, or safety-critical equipment.

04 / Observation log

Temporary, on-screen statuses only. No credentials, Bluetooth identifiers, IR codes, or logs are uploaded or stored by this page.
