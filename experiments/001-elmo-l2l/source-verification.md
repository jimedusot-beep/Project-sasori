Keep real credentials, their MD5 values, session challenges/responses, device identifiers and private captures out of public vectors. Do not fill gaps by choosing familiar checksum defaults, treating notifications as complete frames, replaying historical RX, sending guessed values, clearing app data or factory-resetting the toy.

## Offline result and independent development

The [historical corpus](fixtures/historical-v4.json) preserves E1's exact three byte strings, original evidence class, immutable document/blob reference, lengths and SHA-256 values computed over each byte sequence. These hashes detect changes to the transcription; they do not authenticate an original capture. Tests pin lossless display and the existing decoder's conservative text descriptions, including DataView offsets. They do not assign opcodes.

The [offline capture reviewer](offline-review.md) checks existing Sasori session envelopes, distinguishes omitted/incomplete payloads, recounts patterns from complete bytes and emits counts with fixed labels. It always reports the application protocol verdict as inconclusive. No application-message, password or challenge constructor is implemented or tested: exact source behavior and expected vectors are missing.

The [live experiment draft](live-experiment.md) is **NOT READY**. Its prerequisite gaps stay explicit. Replacement-controller simulation, electrical identification, measured motor limits and mechanical work can proceed independently. Nothing in this track is imported by the website or a replacement-controller runtime; CI requires no Elmo, credentials, archived app or radio connection. Original-board BLE success is not a prerequisite for independently controlled hardware, and BLE UUIDs do not identify motor wiring.
