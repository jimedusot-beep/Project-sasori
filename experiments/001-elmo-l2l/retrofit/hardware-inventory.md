Quantities are design targets, not orders. Exact models, current availability and acquisition cost remain unknown.

| RET-DRV  | Motor power driver / 1 channel if one motor is confirmed | Identified reusable driver only if its interface can be isolated; otherwise a supported external H-bridge | Motor type, driver supply/logic range, transient/current/thermal capability, flyback protection and tested safe output state      | NEEDED FUNCTION; model not selected            |
